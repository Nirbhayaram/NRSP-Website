About NRSP Interior Decorator

We started by fixing one neighbour's window screen

NRSP Interior Decorator began as a small team fitting window screens for houses on our own street. Word travelled because the mesh stayed taut through two monsoons and the fitting didn't warp the window frame — so we kept getting asked to do the next house, then the next office, then a school block.

"A screen only works if it disappears into the window. You should notice the air, not the net."

That idea shaped how we work now. Every job starts with a measurement visit, not a catalogue size, because nylon mesh that's cut a few millimetres short lets more insects in than no screen at all. We cut and fit mosquito nets, mesh doors, mesh curtains and curtain rods ourselves, and we stand behind the fitting, not just the material.

  • Made to measure. Every net, door and rod is cut for the opening it's going into, not a standard size.
  • Fitted by our own installers. No subcontracted labour — the person who measures is accountable for the fit.
  • Built for local conditions. Mesh and frames selected for humidity, dust and monsoon wear.
  • One point of contact. From quote to installation to any adjustment after.
